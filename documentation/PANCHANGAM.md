# Panchangam Concepts

Panchangam means five limbs.

The five limbs used in this project are:

## Tithi

Tithi is based on the angular distance between the Moon and the Sun. Each Tithi spans twelve degrees of separation.

## Vara

Vara is the weekday. In traditional usage, the day is anchored to local sunrise rather than midnight.

## Nakshatra

Nakshatra is the Moon's position among twenty-seven lunar mansions. The app also calculates the Pada.

## Yoga

Yoga is calculated from the combined longitude of the Sun and Moon.

## Karana

Karana is half of a Tithi.

## Why local location matters

Sunrise and sunset differ by location, so the Panchangam for a day can differ by city.
