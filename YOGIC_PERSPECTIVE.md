# Yogic Perspective

Cosmic Analysis treats time as a living field, not only as a civil date system.

The model is:

Time -> Sun Moon Earth geometry -> Panchangam -> body and mind tendencies -> quality of action -> inner awareness.

The project is not fatalistic. It is designed to help a person notice cycles and respond consciously.

## Panchangam

The five limbs are Tithi, Vara, Nakshatra, Yoga, and Karana.

## Muhurta

Muhurta is treated as conscious timing for a chosen activity. The engine ranks time windows and explains the score.

## Personalization

The model can use birth Nakshatra, birth Moon sign, and Lagna to add Tara Bala and Chandra Bala context.
